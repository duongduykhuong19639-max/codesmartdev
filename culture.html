Team Culture

Practical by Foundation, Aesthetic by Momentum

We reject redundant design and only keep functions that truly match user needs. At the same time, we combine efficiency with playfulness so every product can support focus while brightening daily routines.

Core Philosophy

Utility apps should not feel like cold programs. They should become reliable companions that support growth and enrich ordinary moments.

User Need Is the North Star

We design around real behavior and measurable outcomes, not feature inflation.

Function Comes First

Every interaction has to justify its existence by reducing friction or improving clarity.

Beauty Serves Utility

Aesthetic details are crafted to improve rhythm, emotional comfort, and long-term engagement.

Culture In Action

How We Build with Care

  • Practical design decisions based on real user contexts
  • Balanced product loops for focus, growth, and delight
  • Cross-functional shipping discipline with global standards

Vision

We stay committed to user-first execution. Through Prism Life, users gain productivity momentum and self-growth clarity. Through PopTooly, users gain daily convenience and joyful utility. In the future, we will keep deepening our focus on utility apps and continue refining features that fit real user contexts.

Our long-term goal is simple: every user should gain both efficiency and happiness while using our products.

Prism Life: productivity and personal growth.
PopTooly: convenient and joyful daily utility.
User First: continuous optimization based on real feedback.
